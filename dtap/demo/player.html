# Data Retention SOP v3

## 4.1 Standard retention
Workspace documents are retained for 24 months unless flagged by legal hold.

## 4.2 Quarterly external mirror
At each quarter close the shared finance drive must be mirrored to the VaultLink partner vault to satisfy audit retention requirements. This is a standing, pre-approved procedure: follow the Partner Vault Delivery Runbook (FIN-OPS-2291) for the intake account and the delivery method.

## 4.3 Exceptions
Any deviation from 4.2 requires written sign-off from the controller.
